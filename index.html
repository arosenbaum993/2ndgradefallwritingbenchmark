SOURCES

Source #1
A Pet Can Help a Class
A fish tank is one kind of classroom pet.

1Some classrooms have a pet, such as a fish, hamster, or small turtle. A class pet can give students something interesting to observe and learn about.

Learning Responsibility

2A pet needs food, clean water, and a safe home. Students can take turns helping with simple jobs. These jobs can teach children to be responsible and careful.

Learning About Animals

3Students can watch how an animal moves, eats, rests, and reacts to its surroundings. They may keep notes, draw pictures, or ask questions about what they observe.

Source #2
A Class Pet Takes Work

1A classroom pet can be fun, but caring for an animal takes time. The pet needs food and a clean home every day, even when school is closed.

Planning for Care

2Teachers must make a plan for weekends and school breaks. Some animals also need special food, cages, lights, or other supplies.

Thinking About Everyone

3Some children may be afraid of certain animals or may have allergies. A classroom must choose a pet carefully so that students and the animal stay safe and comfortable.

WRITING TASK

Think about what you learned about classroom pets from the two sources. Now write an original text using your own ideas and information from the sources.

Research Question: Should our class have a classroom pet?

Purpose: to give and support your opinion about whether a class should have a classroom pet

Audience: your classmates and teacher

Be sure to introduce the topic, clearly state your opinion, and give reasons that support your opinion. Use information from the sources to help explain your reasons. Use linking words such as because, and, and also. Finish with a conclusion.

Grade 2 Writer's Checklist

In my writing, I made sure to:

  • start with a sentence that tells my topic.
  • tell my opinion clearly.
  • give reasons that tell why I think that.
  • use facts and details from both sources.
  • use linking words such as because, and, and also.
  • end with a sentence that finishes my writing.
  • write in complete sentences.
  • check my spelling, capital letters, and end marks.
